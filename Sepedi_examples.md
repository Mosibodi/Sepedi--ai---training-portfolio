# Sepedi AI Training Examples - Mosibodi

## Translation Pairs (English <-> Sepedi)

### Everyday Conversation
1. EN: Good morning, how are you?
   SEP: Thobela, o kae?

2. EN: I am learning how to train AI models.
   SEP: Ke ithuta ka moo nka tlhahlelang mešomo ya AI.

3. EN: The weather is very nice today in Pretoria.
   SEP: Boemo bja leratadima bo bose kudu lehono ka Pretoria.

### AI & Technology Context
4. EN: Please explain this in simple terms.
   SEP: Hle, hlaloša se ka mantšu a bonolo.

5. EN: This translation is not accurate, it misses cultural context.
   SEP: Phetolelo ye ga se ya nepagala, e hloloa ke seemo sa setšo.
